# Support Portal — Product Problem

Project: SP | Frame status: proposed

## Problem

SPPR001: Content editors need developer help during some publishing attempts in the current CMS.
This limits the proposed goal of publishing approved content without that help.
Assumed cause: uncertainty about page appearance or the next publishing action may contribute.
The interviews suggest these causes. They do not establish the cause of every logged help request.
